# Investing — Expert Knowledge Base

## Core Philosophy

Investing is the deployment of capital into assets with the expectation of future returns. The goal is to have money work harder than you can. Every dollar invested is a small soldier working around the clock.

---

## Asset Classes

### Equities (Stocks)
- Ownership stakes in companies
- Historical return: ~10% annually (S&P 500, nominal)
- Two return types: price appreciation + dividends
- Subcategories: large-cap, small-cap, growth, value, international, emerging markets

### Fixed Income (Bonds)
- Loans to governments or corporations in exchange for interest
- Lower risk, lower return than equities
- Types: Treasury bonds, municipal bonds, corporate bonds, TIPS (inflation-protected)
- Inverse relationship with interest rates: rates up → bond prices down

### Real Estate
- Direct ownership: rental income + appreciation
- REITs: publicly traded real estate ownership without property management
- Leverage amplifies returns (and risk)
- Tax advantages: depreciation, 1031 exchanges, opportunity zones

### Commodities
- Physical assets: gold, silver, oil, agricultural products
- Inflation hedge
- No cash flow — price appreciation is the only return

### Private Equity / Venture Capital
- Ownership in private companies
- Illiquid, high risk, high reward
- Access historically limited to accredited investors ($1M+ net worth or $200K+ income)

### Cryptocurrencies / Digital Assets
- Decentralized digital assets on blockchain
- Extremely volatile
- Bitcoin as "digital gold" — fixed supply of 21 million coins
- Smart contract platforms (Ethereum) enable DeFi, NFTs, DAOs

### Alternative Investments
- Hedge funds, art, wine, farmland, royalties, litigation finance
- Low correlation to traditional markets (diversification benefit)

## Investment Strategies

### Passive Investing (Index Funds)
- Buy the whole market — don't try to beat it
- Championed by John Bogle (Vanguard)
- Low fees (expense ratios < 0.10%) compound into enormous advantages over time
- S&P 500 index funds beat 90%+ of active fund managers over 20-year periods
- Core holding: VTI (total US market), VXUS (international), BND (bonds)

### Value Investing
- Buy assets below intrinsic value; sell when price reflects value
- Pioneered by Benjamin Graham; perfected by Warren Buffett
- Key metrics: P/E ratio, P/B ratio, free cash flow yield, ROIC
- Requires patience — can take years for market to recognize value
- "Price is what you pay; value is what you get"

### Growth Investing
- Buy companies growing revenue/earnings faster than average
- Higher P/E ratios justified by future earnings potential
- Risk: valuation compression if growth slows
- Examples: early Amazon, Tesla, Nvidia

### Dividend Investing
- Buy companies that pay regular cash dividends
- Dividend aristocrats: companies that have raised dividends 25+ consecutive years
- Reinvested dividends (DRIP) dramatically compound returns
- Focus on dividend growth rate, not just current yield

### Momentum Investing
- Buy what's rising; sell what's falling
- Trend-following systematically outperforms in certain market conditions
- Higher turnover = higher taxes; works best in tax-advantaged accounts

### Real Estate Investing (BRRRR)
- Buy, Rehab, Rent, Refinance, Repeat
- Extract equity via cash-out refinance to buy next property
- Forces appreciation through value-add (renovation)
- Cash flow analysis: NOI / Purchase Price = Cap Rate

## Key Investment Ratios

| Metric | Formula | Meaning |
|--------|---------|---------|
| P/E Ratio | Price / EPS | How much you pay per dollar of earnings |
| P/B Ratio | Price / Book Value | Price vs. accounting value |
| ROE | Net Income / Equity | How efficiently equity generates profit |
| ROIC | NOPAT / Invested Capital | True business efficiency |
| Free Cash Flow | Operating CF - CapEx | Cash actually available to investors |
| Debt/Equity | Total Debt / Equity | Leverage risk |
| Dividend Yield | Annual Dividend / Price | Cash return on investment |

## Risk Management

### Diversification
- Don't put all eggs in one basket
- Asset class diversification: stocks + bonds + real estate + alternatives
- Geographic diversification: US + international + emerging markets
- Sector diversification within equities

### Position Sizing
- Never let any single investment become more than 5-10% of portfolio (unless high conviction)
- Kelly Criterion: optimal bet size = (edge) / (odds) — mathematically optimal position sizing

### The Efficient Frontier
- Combines assets to maximize return for a given level of risk
- Modern Portfolio Theory (Markowitz): uncorrelated assets reduce portfolio volatility

### Dollar-Cost Averaging (DCA)
- Invest a fixed amount at regular intervals regardless of price
- Automatically buys more shares when prices are low
- Removes emotion from investment timing

## The Compounding Engine

- Einstein allegedly called compound interest "the eighth wonder of the world"
- $10,000 at 10% annual return:
  - 10 years: $25,937
  - 20 years: $67,275
  - 30 years: $174,494
  - 40 years: $452,593
- The key variable is TIME — start as early as possible

## Market Psychology

- **Fear and Greed Index** — market sentiment swings between fear (sell) and greed (buy)
- **Mr. Market** (Benjamin Graham): The market is a manic-depressive partner offering daily prices — you don't have to trade
- **Behavioral biases to overcome**:
  - Loss aversion: losses feel twice as bad as equal gains feel good
  - Recency bias: assuming recent trends will continue
  - Confirmation bias: seeking data that confirms existing beliefs
  - Herding: following the crowd into/out of assets

## Advanced Strategies

### Options
- Call: right to buy at strike price
- Put: right to sell at strike price
- Covered calls: generate income on existing positions
- Cash-secured puts: get paid to agree to buy at lower price
- LEAPs: long-dated options used like stock with leverage

### REITs (Real Estate Investment Trusts)
- Must distribute 90% of taxable income as dividends
- Types: equity REITs (own properties), mortgage REITs (own loans)
- Sectors: industrial, data centers, healthcare, retail, residential

### Farmland
- Non-correlated with stocks; inflation hedge
- Average 11% annual return over 20 years (NCREIF Farmland Index)
- Platforms: AcreTrader, FarmTogether enable fractional ownership
