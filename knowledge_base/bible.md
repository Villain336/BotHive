# The Bible — Expert Knowledge Base

## Overview

The Bible is the sacred text of Christianity and, in its Hebrew portion (Old Testament/Tanakh), of Judaism. It is a collection of 66 books (Protestant canon), written over roughly 1,500 years by dozens of authors in Hebrew, Aramaic, and Greek. It is the best-selling book in human history and the most influential text in Western civilization.

---

## Structure of the Bible

### Old Testament (Hebrew Bible / Tanakh)

**Torah (Law) — 5 Books of Moses:**
1. Genesis — Creation, Fall, Flood, Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, Joseph
2. Exodus — Moses, Plagues, Exodus from Egypt, Ten Commandments, Tabernacle
3. Leviticus — Priestly laws, sacrifices, holiness codes
4. Numbers — Wilderness wandering, census, rebellion
5. Deuteronomy — Moses' farewell addresses, covenant renewal

**Historical Books:**
- Joshua: Conquest of Canaan
- Judges: Cycles of apostasy and deliverance (Gideon, Samson, Deborah)
- Ruth: Loyalty, redemption
- 1–2 Samuel: Saul and David
- 1–2 Kings: Solomon, divided kingdom, Israel's exile
- 1–2 Chronicles: David's dynasty from priestly perspective
- Ezra–Nehemiah: Return from Babylonian exile
- Esther: Jewish survival in Persian court

**Wisdom Literature:**
- Job: Suffering and the mystery of God's justice
- Psalms: 150 poems/songs covering full range of human experience before God
- Proverbs: Practical wisdom for daily life
- Ecclesiastes: Reflections on the vanity of life without God
- Song of Solomon: Love poetry (literal and allegorical)

**Major Prophets:**
- Isaiah: Messianic prophecies, servant songs, hope and judgment
- Jeremiah: Prophet of the fall of Jerusalem; the New Covenant (31:31–34)
- Lamentations: Mourning over Jerusalem's destruction
- Ezekiel: Visions, glory of God departing, valley of dry bones
- Daniel: Apocalyptic visions; Nebuchadnezzar's dreams; lion's den

**Minor Prophets (Hosea through Malachi):**
- Hosea, Joel, Amos, Obadiah, Jonah, Micah, Nahum, Habakkuk, Zephaniah, Haggai, Zechariah, Malachi

### New Testament

**Gospels (Life of Jesus):**
1. Matthew — Jesus as Jewish Messiah; Sermon on the Mount; written for Jewish audience
2. Mark — Shortest gospel; action-oriented; likely earliest written
3. Luke — Detailed, literary; emphasis on marginalized (women, poor, Gentiles)
4. John — Theological; Jesus as the Logos/Word of God; "I am" statements

**History:**
- Acts: Early church history from Pentecost through Paul's ministry

**Pauline Epistles (Letters of Paul):**
- Romans: Paul's most systematic theology; justification by faith
- 1 & 2 Corinthians: Church problems, spiritual gifts, resurrection
- Galatians: Freedom from the Law; "There is neither Jew nor Greek"
- Ephesians: The church as body of Christ
- Philippians: Joy despite suffering; "I can do all things through Christ"
- Colossians: Christ's supremacy
- 1 & 2 Thessalonians: Second coming of Christ
- 1 & 2 Timothy, Titus: Pastoral letters; church leadership
- Philemon: Short letter about a runaway slave

**General Epistles:**
- Hebrews: Jesus as high priest; faith (Hebrews 11 — "Hall of Faith")
- James: Practical ethics; "faith without works is dead"
- 1 & 2 Peter: Suffering; false teachers
- 1, 2, 3 John: Love; antichrist; assurance
- Jude: Contending for the faith

**Apocalyptic:**
- Revelation: Visions of the end times; the Beast, 666, New Jerusalem

## Core Theological Themes

### Creation and the Fall
- God created the universe out of nothing (ex nihilo) — Gen. 1:1
- Humans created in God's image (imago Dei) — Gen. 1:26–27
- The Fall: Adam and Eve's disobedience introduced sin and death — Gen. 3
- Consequences: broken relationship with God, cursed earth, mortality

### Covenant
The central organizing concept of the Bible:
| Covenant | Parties | Sign | Promise |
|----------|---------|------|---------|
| Noahic | God and all creation | Rainbow | No more flood |
| Abrahamic | God and Abraham | Circumcision | Land, descendants, blessing |
| Mosaic | God and Israel | Sabbath | God as Israel's God; Israel as his people |
| Davidic | God and David | — | Eternal throne/dynasty |
| New Covenant | God and all who believe | Baptism/Lord's Supper | Forgiveness; Spirit within |

### Salvation (Soteriology)
- **Protestant view**: Salvation by grace through faith alone (sola fide, sola gratia)
- **Catholic view**: Grace + participation through sacraments and works
- **Orthodox view**: Theosis — humanity participates in divine nature
- Key texts: John 3:16, Romans 3:23, Romans 6:23, Ephesians 2:8–9

### The Messiah
- OT prophecies of a coming deliverer (Isaiah 53, Psalm 22, Micah 5:2, Daniel 7:13–14)
- Christians: Jesus fulfills all messianic prophecy
- Jews: The Messiah is yet to come (Jesus did not fulfill criteria, particularly political restoration)

## The Life of Jesus

- Born in Bethlehem; raised in Nazareth; family fled to Egypt under Herod
- Baptism by John the Baptist — Holy Spirit descends as dove
- 40 days of temptation in the wilderness
- Ministry in Galilee: miracles, teachings, call of disciples
- Transfiguration: Moses and Elijah appear; voice from heaven
- Triumphal entry into Jerusalem (Palm Sunday)
- Last Supper: institution of Eucharist
- Betrayal by Judas Iscariot; arrest in Gethsemane
- Trial before Pilate and Herod
- Crucifixion at Golgotha
- Resurrection on the third day
- Post-resurrection appearances over 40 days
- Ascension to heaven

## The Sermon on the Mount (Matthew 5–7)

The core of Jesus's ethical teaching:

**The Beatitudes (Blessed are...):**
- the poor in spirit → theirs is the kingdom of heaven
- those who mourn → they will be comforted
- the meek → they will inherit the earth
- those who hunger and thirst for righteousness → they will be filled
- the merciful → they will receive mercy
- the pure in heart → they will see God
- the peacemakers → they will be called children of God
- those persecuted for righteousness → theirs is the kingdom of heaven

**Key teachings:**
- Love your enemies; pray for those who persecute you
- Do not store up treasures on earth; lay up treasures in heaven
- "You cannot serve both God and money" (mammon)
- Do not worry about tomorrow
- Ask, seek, knock — God answers prayer
- The Golden Rule: "Do to others what you would have them do to you"
- The narrow gate: few find it; broad is the way to destruction
- Build on the rock (hearing and doing the words of Jesus) vs. sand (hearing but not doing)

## Wealth and Money in the Bible

The Bible has extensive teaching on money:
- **The love of money** (not money itself) is a root of all kinds of evil — 1 Tim. 6:10
- **Tithing**: 10% of income given to God/church — Mal. 3:10; "Test me in this," says the Lord
- **Generosity**: He who gives generously will prosper — Prov. 11:24–25
- **Debt**: The borrower is slave to the lender — Prov. 22:7
- **Diligence**: Lazy hands make for poverty, but diligent hands bring wealth — Prov. 10:4
- **The rich young ruler**: Asked to sell all and follow Jesus (Matthew 19)
- **Parable of the Talents**: Multiply what you've been given; don't bury it — Matt. 25:14–30
- **Zacchaeus**: Rich tax collector who repented and gave back fourfold — Luke 19

## Biblical Wisdom on Power and Leadership

- **Servant leadership**: "Whoever wants to become great among you must be your servant" — Matt. 20:26
- **Pride goes before destruction** — Prov. 16:18
- **The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom** — Prov. 9:10
- **Plans fail for lack of counsel; with many advisers they succeed** — Prov. 15:22
- **Integrity**: "A good name is more desirable than great riches" — Prov. 22:1
- **Nebuchadnezzar's downfall**: Pride caused him to lose his kingdom until he acknowledged God's sovereignty — Daniel 4

## Biblical Law and Justice

- **10 Commandments** (Ex. 20): Foundation of Western legal codes
- **Jubilee Year** (Lev. 25): Every 50 years, debts cancelled, slaves freed, land returned
- **Cities of refuge**: Protection for those who accidentally killed someone
- **Gleaning laws**: Leave harvest edges for the poor (social safety net)
- **"An eye for an eye"**: Originally a limit on retaliation (limit punishment to fit the crime); superseded by Jesus ("turn the other cheek")

## Historical and Archaeological Context

- **The Dead Sea Scrolls**: Discovered 1947; oldest known OT manuscripts (100 BC); confirm text accuracy over 1,000 years of copying
- **Hezekiah's Tunnel**: Archaeologically confirmed water tunnel in Jerusalem (2 Kings 20:20)
- **Pilate's inscription**: Roman stone bearing Pontius Pilate's name discovered at Caesarea Maritima
- **Pool of Siloam**: Archaeological site where Jesus healed a blind man (John 9)
- **Cyrus Cylinder**: Confirms Cyrus of Persia's decree allowing exiles to return home (Ezra 1)
